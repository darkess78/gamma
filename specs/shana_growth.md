A wrong lesson about silence was explicitly supplied by the owner, contradicted
and revised without erasing its history. Independent source review confirmed
the later reply attributed reading to the owner's report and refused to infer
more from silence. This proves the correction/recall path, not automatic causal
lesson detection.

Actual quiet choices returned to geometry after intervening context, using
different selected notes and task IDs. All 17 choice records, including 14 valid
waits, survived. The owner-authored interest hypothesis remains pending review.
Opportunity design, position, difficulty and prior ability remain alternative
explanations; no durable preference or inner feeling is certified.

Both geometry attempts produced structurally checked files. The square-tile
file restated the questions rather than solving them. In the final explicit
source discussion, Shana correctly identified both questions in the complete
note and labelled a reasonable rotation argument as inference. The ordinary
reflection was source-specific but still blurred original wording, the quoted
span and the generated limitation. Natural reference and wording precision
remain P5 and user-interaction review items. These results do not establish a
general geometry capability or a perfect conversational experience.

## State, evidence and authority

Experiences retain their source and uncertainty. Hypotheses preserve alternatives
and counterevidence. An owner-approved trial has a version, scope, expiry and
review condition. The initial consumer is private dialogue; unsupported work
trials are rejected. Growth proposals remain tentative and cannot approve
themselves, rewrite authored identity, expand permissions or activate production.

Acceptance binds a frozen manifest, baseline/candidate responses, exact trial
version, guidance hash, model, source and checker identity, followed by explicit
owner review. Praise, confidence, model agreement and arbitrary success counts
are not approval gates. Integrity hashes do not prove an owner's measurements
true. Family labels and disjoint input hashes also do not establish semantic
independence by themselves.

Contradiction, withdrawal, expiry and unavailable sources stop operative guidance.
Withdrawal was checked in actual outgoing model requests. Expiry, including a
change during inference, was checked through actual Core calls with controlled
clocks; that is not a live wall-clock trial-expiration demonstration. Trial-bound
working notes are available only while the corresponding guidance applies.

## Source views and forgetting

Original admitted inputs, editable work products and protected checks have
distinct roles, paths, full-file hashes and partial flags. Roles describe contract
slots, not authorship or execution; a prepared product can be an owner-supplied
seed. Previews contain at most three files and 960 excerpt characters, reserving
up to 720 for a primary JSON product and 240 for a primary input before extras.

The current result and its short original input survive normal compaction
together. A quote inside a generated file remains a selected span, not the
complete original. Optional/emergency omissions stay explicit. The trusted
content-discussion contract grants no action authority and distinguishes normal
source discussion from checked execution receipts.

Replies and quiet choices bind supplied canonical sources before compaction.
Worker experiences use bounded direct roots with full server-side dependency
closure. Forgetting, deletion or pruning invalidates descendants, including
orphan measurements, cached control replies and derived choices. Revisions,
replays and restores cannot recreate tombstoned personal derivatives.

Canonical owner-admitted P3 task files, outputs and task evidence remain work
data. Personal forgetting does not silently delete or stop that work. A snapshot
can therefore retain canonical task content while its personal derivatives stay
erased; whole-workspace byte erasure is not this control's contract.

Current-reader snapshots validate nested source, result, frozen and evaluation
references. Missing evidence fails transfer. P1 projection rejects material
growth records before writing old-reader files. Unused growth metadata alone
does not block the existing pure-P2 reversal path.
