## Scope deliberately left open

The following are future choices, not implied requirements for the first release:
permanent ignoring of a person; autonomous plans with others or joining their
channels; incoming events waking Shana; independently choosing a whole stream
concept; continuous raw audiovisual archives; particular model architectures,
training techniques or paid providers; unlimited tools, spending, publication or
live self-modification. Android, OSRS companionship and both private stream
formats are selected. The owner deferred real Minecraft testing. Acceptable
latency, retention, wake schedules, visual direction and subjective interaction
quality still need their relevant decisions or acceptance.

The full goal is reached through accepted experiences across these rows, with
remaining future choices clearly labeled. A release can deliver a useful subset;
it must say which rows are accepted, partial, unproved, deferred or superseded.
